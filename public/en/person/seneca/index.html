Seneca the Younger

루키우스 안나이우스 세네카
Philosopher and Statesman · 4 BCE–65
Explore places on the map

What they left

He wrote Stoic ethics as letters and everyday cases rather than school doctrine, and redefined the length of a life as a question of how time is used. Tutor to Nero and close to power, he was finally ordered by Nero to kill himself.
