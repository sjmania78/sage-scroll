Xunzi

순자
Confucian Philosopher · 310 BCE–238 BCE
Explore places on the map

What they left

Against Mencius he held that human nature is bad and that goodness is produced by learning and ritual. He treated heaven as natural process rather than a personal power, and Han Feizi and Li Si came out of his school.
