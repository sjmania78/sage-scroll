Buddhist Philosopher · 150–250

What they left

He established emptiness by argument: nothing holds by an essence of its own, only in dependence on conditions. That Madhyamaka reasoning became the theoretical base of Mahayana Buddhism across Tibet, China, Korea and Japan.
