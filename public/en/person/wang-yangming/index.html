Wang Yangming

왕양명
Philosopher and Official · 1472–1529
Explore places on the map

What they left

Against Zhu Xi's method of seeking principle in external things, he held that mind is principle and that knowing and acting are not separable. The claim carried through late imperial East Asia and reached the activists of late-Tokugawa Japan.
