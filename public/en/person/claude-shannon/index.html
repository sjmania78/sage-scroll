Claude Shannon

클로드 섀넌
Mathematician & computer scientist · 1916–2001
Explore places on the map

What they left

His 1948 paper measured information in bits and computed the capacity of a noisy channel. Earlier he had shown that switching circuits could be handled as Boolean algebra, laying the ground for digital design.
