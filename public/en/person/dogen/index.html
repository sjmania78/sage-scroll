Dōgen

도겐
Buddhist Philosopher and Monk · 1200–1253
Explore places on the map

What they left

He taught shikantaza — that sitting is itself awakening — and wrote his major work in Japanese rather than Chinese. The Sōtō school of Japanese Zen continued from the monastery he founded at Eiheiji.
