John Dewey

Philosopher and Educator · 1859–1952

What they left

He treated thinking as an instrument for solving problems rather than a mirror of truth, and redefined education as learning a shared way of living rather than receiving knowledge. He opened a school to test the theory, and its design stayed in the twentieth-century classroom.
