Li Zhi

이지(이탁오)
Philosopher and Writer · 1527–1602
Explore places on the map

What they left

He rejected the right and wrong fixed by neo-Confucian orthodoxy and set the unadorned childlike mind as the standard of judgement. He titled his book as one to be burned, it was duly banned, and he died in prison.
