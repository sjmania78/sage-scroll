Immanuel Kant

이마누엘 칸트
Philosopher · 1724–1804
Explore places on the map

What they left

He reversed the relation of mind and world — objects conform to the forms of cognition, not the other way round — and reset the baseline of modern philosophy. He grounded morality in principles that could hold for anyone, and proposed a federation of republics to end war.
