Philosopher (Daoism) · 369 BCE–286 BCE

What they left

He wrote philosophy in fables and jokes, arguing that the standard dividing right from wrong shifts with where one stands. The butterfly dream and its way of unsettling the boundaries of knowing carried into East Asian poetry, painting and Chan Buddhism.
