Sigmund Freud

지크문트 프로이트
Founder of Psychoanalysis · 1856–1939
Explore places on the map

What they left

He held that much of what people do comes from motives they cannot see, and built a method for reading dreams and slips as routes to them. Individual doctrines were widely contested afterwards, but the premise of the unconscious stayed in twentieth-century culture.
